Fit

  • High on the chest and upper back, never on the stomach
  • Straps tight: jog three steps in place, nothing bounces
  • Shorter stride, eyes on the horizon
  • Sore neck or flushed face after = too low or too heavy

Ask a doctor or physio first if

  • Neck or back pain, or a disc problem
  • Hip or knee arthritis, or osteoporosis
  • Pregnant, under 6 months postpartum, pelvic floor symptoms
  • High blood pressure, heart condition, or a fall in the last year

Stop a walk on new joint pain, breathlessness that does not settle, dizziness, or numbness. General information for healthy adults, not medical advice.
