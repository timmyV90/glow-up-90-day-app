Add minutes before you add weight. Cap at 10–15% of bodyweight. Never run in the vest.

Why 5 to 10%?

At 10% of bodyweight a walk uses roughly 10% more energy than without the vest, and your heart rate runs 15–25 beats higher. Above 10–15% the load on your joints rises faster than the benefit. The "starter" 12 lb vest is over 10% for anyone under 55 kg / 120 lb.

The plan, in short

3 walks a week, a rest day between them. Loads are a percentage of your bodyweight. Walks are counted, not days: miss one, do the next.

Week 1 5% · 3 × 20 min, flat, easy pace
Week 2 5% · 3 × 25–30 min
Week 3 6.5% · 3 × 25 min (minutes drop when load rises)
Week 4 6.5% · 3 × 30 min, a few hills
Week 5 · hold 6.5% · 3 × 30–35 min. Your joints catch up with your lungs.
Week 6 8% · 3 × 30 min, flat
Week 7 8% · 3 × 35 min, hills welcome
Week 8 10% · 3 × 30 min. The finish line.

Two rules

1. Add minutes before you add weight. Every week that raises the load keeps the minutes the same or lower.

2. Pain two walks in a row → drop one step. The app does this for you: tick the ache boxes when you log, and the next target goes back a step until you have two pain-free walks.

Before you start

Talk to a doctor or physio first if you have neck or back pain, a disc problem, hip or knee arthritis, osteoporosis, are pregnant or under 6 months postpartum, have pelvic floor symptoms, uncontrolled high blood pressure, a heart condition, or have fallen in the last year. Stop a walk on new joint pain, breathlessness that does not settle, dizziness, or numbness and tingling.

General information for healthy adults, based on published guidance. Not medical advice.

Fit

Weight high and tight on the upper back and chest. Pull the straps until nothing bounces when you jog three steps in place. Shorter, quicker stride, eyes on the horizon. A sore neck or a flushed face after a walk means the vest sits too low or is too heavy for today.
